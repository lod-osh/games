HOW TO PLAY

◈ COMBAT

Turn-based tactics on a 20×20 grid. Click one of your dark-colored units to select it, then use action buttons. Each unit gets one move and one attack per turn. When all units have acted, the turn ends automatically (or press End Turn early). You start with 150 deployment credits.

Move — highlights reachable cells in blue. Click to move. Attack — highlights enemies in red within range + line of sight. Watch — sets overwatch: the unit will fire at any enemy that moves through its range. Reload — refills ammo. Melee — engage an adjacent enemy in hand-to-hand combat with a timing meter.

◈ MELEE COMBAT

Choose Light Attack (slow needle, 1 base dmg) or Heavy Attack (fast needle, 2 base dmg). Stop the needle in the center zone for maximum power. After you attack, the enemy attacks and you must block using the same meter. Skills like Haymaker, Iron Block, and Counter enhance melee.

◈ COVER & LINE OF SIGHT

Units behind walls get cover (reduced enemy hit chance). Line of sight is blocked by walls, enemy units, and civilians — you can't shoot through them. Friendly units don't block your shots. Use walls strategically for protection.

◈ CIVILIANS

Yellow sprites are civilians. Move adjacent to rescue them for credits and street rep. They move on their own during the civilian phase. Attacking civilians makes them hostile and unrescueable.

◈ FOG OF WAR

You can only see within your units' sight range. Enemies hidden in fog can still shoot you. The 👁 icon means an enemy has spotted your unit. Night Vision skill extends sight range. Eagle Eye spots enemies at double distance.

◈ SKILLS

After each level, spend shared XP to learn skills. Skills form trees — some require prerequisites. Categories: Ranged (Sharpshooter, Marksman, Suppression), Melee (Brawler → Haymaker, Iron Block → Counter, Charge), Mobility (Sprint, Zig-Zag), Support (Field Medic, Rescue, Scavenger), Tactical (Breacher, Night Vision, Eagle Eye, Last Stand).

◈ EXPLORE MODE

Between missions, explore the location. Talk to NPCs, collect quest items, sell organs to merchants, scavenge for credits, do odd jobs, and play blackjack. Three merchant tiers: Organ Merchant (standard price), Premium Buyer (premium, good+ only), and Scrap Dealer (cheap, buys anything). You can haggle for better prices. Hospitalize fallen units at the Infirmary between missions.

◈ ORGANS

Killing enemies may harvest organs in 5 conditions: ruined, poor, fair, good, perfect. Better condition = higher price. Scavenger skill improves harvest quality. Prices scale with level.

◈ TRANSIT MAP

After exploring, the transit map lets you choose your next mission. Cleared locations can be revisited for more scavenging and trading. The game auto-saves after each victory.

◈ TACTICAL BONUSES

Flanking — If two of your units can see the same enemy from different angles (90°+ apart), attacks on that enemy get +25% accuracy and +1 damage. A ✕ icon appears on flanked targets. The Flanker skill doubles the accuracy bonus to +50%.

Coordinated Fire — When multiple units attack the same enemy in one turn, each subsequent attack gets +10% accuracy per previous attacker (max +30%). The Coordinator skill adds another +25% on top.

First Strike — Attacking an enemy that just became visible (wasn't seen last turn) gives +20% accuracy. The Ambusher skill increases this to +50%. Position your units near fog edges and let enemies walk into view.

Overwatch — Units on overwatch fire at any enemy that moves through their range. Use this to control corridors and doorways. The Zig-Zag skill halves overwatch accuracy against your moving units.

◈ STRATEGY TIPS

Split your squad to create flanking angles — two units on opposite sides of an enemy are devastating. Focus fire on one target at a time for coordinated fire bonuses. Use fog of war to your advantage: position near corners and let enemies walk into first-strike range. Rescue civilians early — they move unpredictably and can block line of sight. Save credits for hospitalization. Invest in Scavenger early for better organ income. Melee is risky but Brawler + Haymaker makes it devastating. Night Vision and Eagle Eye give huge tactical advantage in fog. On higher levels, enemies will try to flank you — don't cluster your units together.
