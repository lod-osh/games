WELCOME TO CLUB KISSFIST

Every midnight, the city's outcasts descend into an abandoned subway tunnel for bare-knuckle fights.

CHALLENGE fighters to climb the ranks. Win XP and cash.

THE BAR — Buy drinks to boost stats. The bartender knows secrets.

TIMING — Tap when the slider hits the green zone. Dead center = CRITICAL hit.

FATIGUE — Fighting takes a toll. Each fight this night adds fatigue, reducing your starting HP and stamina. Go home to reset.

KNOCKDOWNS — When you knock someone down, a follow-up window opens. Strike fast for bonus damage.

FIGHTER TRAITS — Higher-ranked opponents have unique mechanics. Learn their patterns or get destroyed.

RANK GATES — Some opponents won't fight you until you've earned it. The Punchmaster decides.
